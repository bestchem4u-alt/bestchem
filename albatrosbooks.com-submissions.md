# Submissions

[![](https://www.albatrosbooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/sirsi-1024x359.jpg)](https://www.albatrosbooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/sirsi-1024x359.jpg)

### What We’re Looking For

**Albatros Books** is a leading independent children’s book publisher focused on **playful, educational, and inspiring books for kids and young readers**. Our mission is  
to publish exceptional titles that spark **curiosity, creativity, and a lifelong love of reading** in children.

At Albatros Books, we publish a diverse range of children’s literature, including:

- **Picture books**
- **Board books**
- **Chapter books**
- **Middle-grade fiction**
- **Nonfiction for kids**
- **Novelty books**

We are currently seeking high-quality manuscripts with characters and storylines that children themselves will be interested in, especially books that will make them  
laugh, wonder, feel deeply, and see the world in new ways. We value excellent storytelling with a strong point of view.

### We are especially interested in:

- **Funny, clever, and shareable books** for young readers
- **Joyful and imaginative stories** that ignite creativity
- **Children’s mysteries** (but please, no Sherlock Holmes retellings)
- **Innovative early reader books** beyond the “two friends” trope
- **Diverse voices and cultural perspectives**, especially when paired with engaging, humorous, or thrilling content
- **High-interest fiction and nonfiction** that keeps kids and tweens turning the pages
- **Light magical realism**, but not epic fantasy
- **Real-world topics** that kids care about, told in fresh, accessible ways
- **Well-sourced, innovative nonfiction** for kids, including STEM, social issues, and more
- **Memoirs and personal narratives** with a strong, unique voice

If you’re writing for children and want to publish with a press that prioritizes **impactful storytelling**, **diverse representation**, and **reader engagement**, we want  
to hear from you.

Before submitting, we encourage you to get to know our books and our mission. Understanding what we publish is the best way to see if your work is a strong fit.  
Please note that we do not accept any material created by AI.

### Tips How to Submit

To submit your work send us the following as **a single PDF or Word document**:

- A **one-page query letter**
- Your **full manuscript** (for picture books) or
- The **first 2–3 chapters + a brief synopsis** (for longer works)
- **Formatting requirements**: Double-spaced, Standard 12-point font (Times New Roman or similar)

**Email submissions** **to:** [submissions@albatrosbooks.com](mailto:submissions@albatrosbooks.com)

### Tips on Writing a Strong Query Letter

A great **query letter** helps your manuscript stand out. Here’s what to include:

- A short and compelling summary of your book
- What makes your story **unique or marketable**
- Target age range (e.g., ages 4–8, middle grade 8–12, etc.)
- How your book fits into today’s **children’s book market**
- Any relevant **author background** or **expertise**
- Mention your **social media presence**, **author platform**, or any **marketing ideas**
- Please avoid describing your book as “the next Harry Potter” or including feedback from family or friends

### What to Expect After Submission

While we read every submission with care, due to the high volume we receive, we are only able to respond to submissions that we are interested in pursuing further.  
If we feel your manuscript is a good fit, we will reach out to you directly.

### Get to Know Us Before You Submit

We strongly recommend exploring our published titles and reading about our **editorial mission** to see if your manuscript aligns with our vision. Submitting a manuscript  
tailored to our **publishing style** increases your chances of success.

We can’t wait to discover your story. Thank you for considering **Albatros Books**, a trusted name in **quality children’s publishing**.
